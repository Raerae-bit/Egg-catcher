How to Play 🐥

Move the baby chick left and right to catch the falling eggs.

Each caught egg earns 10 points. Every egg you miss costs one life.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or A / D. Press P or Esc to pause.

Mobile: Hold the left and right buttons.
